\begin{frame}{Black-Scholes PDE}

Let \(P(S,t)\) be the value of the European put.

\[
\frac{\partial P}{\partial t}
+\frac{1}{2}\sigma^{2}S^{2}\frac{\partial^{2}P}{\partial S^{2}}
+rS\frac{\partial P}{\partial S}
-rP=0

need to define all terms P, \sigma, S, etc
\]

\end{frame}
